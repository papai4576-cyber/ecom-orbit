# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Skeptical Indian SMB founders, mostly on mobile (80%+ of Indian ecommerce traffic). Two groups: D2C founders doing ₹5L–₹1Cr/month who need to scale across several channels, and offline retailers (local shops, regional businesses) going online for the first time. Their job on this site: decide whether Ecom Orbit is trustworthy enough to book a call or submit the contact form.

## Product Purpose

Ecom Orbit is an India-only ecommerce agency. The website is its main lead-generation and credibility asset. Success is a visitor submitting the contact form or booking a consultation via the primary CTA "Get Your Free Ecommerce Audit".

## Positioning

A founder-led, two-person agency covering 7 channels under one team (store setup, performance marketing, SEO, marketplaces, quick commerce, logistics, end-to-end management). Visitors get direct access to the founders, with no account managers or junior handoffs. It serves brands too big for freelancers and too small for enterprise agencies.

## Operating Context

Platforms the team works on: Shopify, WooCommerce, Meta, Google, Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Nykaa, Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, Shiprocket, Delhivery, Blue Dart. India-native numbers are required: ₹, lakhs and crores, Indian date format.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js site on Vercel, live at ecomorbit.in. Contact form posts to a Google Sheets webhook.
- WhatsApp CTA is disabled site-wide. Do not re-add it without a real business number.
- Primary CTA text is always "Get Your Free Ecommerce Audit".
- Do not claim platform partner or certification status; "Specialists" only.
- Avoid "only agency" exclusivity claims. Response time is 24 hours.
- Do not lead headline copy with "D2C"; the site serves offline retailers too.
- Performance target: 90+ PageSpeed, lazy-load below the fold.

## Brand Commitments

- The existing logo (Brand Assets/Brand_logo.png, orange) stays unchanged.
- The design follows the cinematic night direction in DESIGN.md (starfield, orbit scenes, glass UI, light-weight type, single orange accent), set by the owner from a reference on 2026-10-28; it replaced the earlier Apple-style direction. Stats stay in JetBrains Mono.
- Voice: direct, confident, India-native; "a sharp, experienced friend who runs an ecommerce agency". Avoid "world-class", "cutting-edge", "leverage synergies".

## Evidence on Hand

- Per-engagement numbers exist in agents_docs/brand-context.md, but the prior case studies and founder testimonials were placeholder and have been removed site-wide. No real testimonials, client logos or case studies exist yet; do not fabricate them.
- About 10–12 brands served in total. Do not display aggregate scale numbers.
- Pricing tiers are published (Starter, Growth, Scale).

## Product Principles

1. Trust before polish: every claim must be true and checkable; specifics over superlatives.
2. Mobile first, rupees and ROAS first.
3. One clear action on every screen: book the free audit.
4. Founders are the proof; show the people and the process, not invented scale.
