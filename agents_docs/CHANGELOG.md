# Changelog — Ecom Orbit Website

*Running record of shipped changes, newest first. One entry per change with a one-line reason. Update this in the same pass as the change itself — see "Single Source of Truth" in `CLAUDE.md`.*

---

- **Mobile fixes: no zoomed-out load, tidier alignment, no strike-through (2026-10-28):** Owner: dislikes the strike effect on pain points; some mobile sections misaligned; site opens "a little zoomed" on phones. Cause of the zoom: decorative glows (section-head haze at 130% width, hero/CTA rings) spilled past the screen edge, so phones widened the layout viewport (406px on a 375px screen) and shrank the page. Fix: `overflow-x: clip` on `html` and `body` (keeps sticky scroll working), section-head haze capped at 100%, explicit `viewport` export (device-width, scale 1, theme colour). Replaced `StrikeList` with `PainList` (numbered lines, orange mono index, hairlines) on all 7 service pages. Hero stats are a tidy 2x2 grid on mobile instead of a 3+1 wrap, hero orbit is inset from the screen edge, and the Orbit Scrub channel text is centred under its centred heading on mobile. Checked every inner page: viewport equals screen width, no text outside the 20px gutters. *Why: the page loaded smaller than the phone and a few blocks sat off-axis.*
- **Minimal treatment for every inner page (2026-10-28):** Owner asked for the same minimal, varied, visual treatment on all pages. New shared kit (`components/page/`, `page-hero.tsx`, `cta-section.tsx`): left-aligned minimal hero with a CSS `OrbitMark` picture, scroll-lit text, struck-through pain list, pill cloud, tick rows, big-number stat row, FAQ accordion, interactive link list, next-page link, pulsing-ring CTA. Rebuilt `/services`, `/services/[slug]` (x7), `/industries`, `/industries/[slug]` (x4), `/about`, `/blog`, `/blog/[slug]`, `/contact`, `/free-audit`; legal pages inherit the new hero (text unchanged). Added `tagline`/`stats` (services) and `tagline`/`stat` (industries) to the data and cut visible copy sharply; dropped unsourced market-size eyebrows from industry heroes. The page shell no longer pads for the old fixed bar. *Why: only the homepage had been redone; inner pages were still heading-paragraph-card-grid.*
- **One idea per place, "one team" removed from repeats (2026-10-28):** Owner: "don't repeat the one team concept every time." It appeared in the hero line, Orbit Scrub heading and End-to-End text, nav dropdown, services index headline and subheadline, and About meta. Kept it only in the homepage Statement and the End-to-End service page. New copy: hero "Stores, marketplaces and quick commerce for Indian brands."; Orbit Scrub "Every place your customers buy." (eyebrow "Seven channels"); End-to-End panel "run together and reported monthly"; nav "All 7 channels, one monthly report"; services index "Everything your ecommerce brand needs. Every channel." Added a message rule to `content-copy.md`. *Why: the same pitch in every section reads as filler.*
- **Minimal hero (2026-10-28):** Owner: hero too text heavy, look for inspiration. Compared Linear, Raycast, Resend and Vercel heroes (short headline, at most two lines of support, one dominant object). Cut the hero to headline + one line + one button + quiet link; removed the badge, paragraph, specialties caption and the stat cards (stats are now one mono line). The Orbit Scene is the whole picture: bigger ring, depth-of-field blur on back orbs, a comet of sparks along the orbit, mouse-tilt parallax. *Why: the hero carried six text elements and a card row; the references carry one picture and almost no words.*
- **Minimal homepage pass (2026-10-28):** Owner: "every section has a lot of text, most sections look similar, I want minimalistic, look for design inspiration." Studied Linear and Raycast (one short line + one dominant visual per section; a different kind of object each time) and rebuilt the homepage from 11 sections to 9, each a distinct layout: scroll-lit statement, outlined platform marquee, industry card rail, scroll-filled process path, quick-commerce/marketplace bento with an animated 10-min ring and self-ticking checklist, open-column pricing, pulsing-ring final CTA. Deleted Multi-Channel, Services Grid, Problem, Industry Verticals, Process Timeline, QC and Marketplace Spotlight (services already live in the Orbit Scrub). Copy cut roughly 80% (see `content-copy.md`); dropped the unsourced market-size stats and the contradictory "Dedicated account manager" pricing line. Looping animations stop under reduced motion. *Why: sections read as the same heading-paragraph-card-grid repeated, which is the opposite of minimal.*
- **Reversed logo for the dark theme (2026-10-28):** the first night pass put the light-backdrop logo in a white chip, which looked like a patch. Generated `public/logo-reversed.png` from the master (backdrop removed, navy to light, orange and shapes unchanged) and dropped the chip from nav and footer. *Why: owner said the white patch looked very bad.*
- **Cinematic night redesign (2026-10-28):** Owner shared a dark, atmospheric reference ("current site is very text based, need more visuals and motion, better UI/UX"). Re-themed the whole site by re-pointing the tone tokens: deep navy-black base, fixed canvas starfield with scroll parallax, translucent glass cards (20px, pointer-following orange highlight), light-weight (300) display type with orange emphasis phrases, glass secondary buttons. New floating glass-pill nav on a top scrim (logo sits in a rounded light chip since the master file is navy on light). New hero: glass badge, headline, and a code-built **Orbit Scene** (7 channel orbs circling a glowing hub with depth/occlusion) with a glass facts rail; the four stats moved from the old StatsStrip into that rail (`stats-strip.tsx` deleted). Services and Industries grids are swipe carousels on mobile; section heads get a soft haze; mobile sticky "Get Your Free Ecommerce Audit" pill after the hero. No imagery added: no generated/stock images or testimonials (none are real). Updated `DESIGN.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `PRODUCT.md`. *Why: the Apple-style system read flat and text-heavy; the owner wanted a more cinematic, visual, motion-rich feel while keeping every claim checkable.*
- **Scroll-driven homepage upgrade (2026-10-28):** Added the Orbit Scrub, a pinned dark section directly under the hero where scrolling walks the orbit through all 7 channels (copy reused from `data/services.ts`; End-to-End line reworded to drop the WhatsApp reference). Hero now has scroll depth (copy drifts up/softens, orbit rises/grows). Process-timeline step rules draw in orange on view. Code-only, no generated imagery or video; reduced-motion gets a static card grid. Updated `DESIGN.md` (pinned scroll now allowed for this one section; home rhythm). *Why: owner asked for a better, more memorable design using the scroll-craft approach; free stock footage/generated video were ruled out on cost, so depth and motion are built in code.*
- **Full visual redesign (2026-10-27):** Replaced the dark-navy "Mission Control" look with an Apple-style system: alternating white / light-gray / black sections, logo orange as the only accent, SF Pro (Inter fallback) + JetBrains Mono, pill CTAs, borderless cards, light glass nav, light footer. Rewrote every component and page; removed the ticker, browser mockup, pinned-scroll timeline, parallax layer, emoji, teal/purple/red accents, glows and grid backgrounds. Hero now shows the 7-channel orbit diagram. Added `PRODUCT.md`, new root `DESIGN.md`, moved the Apple reference to `agents_docs/reference-apple-design.md`, updated `CLAUDE.md` non-negotiables. Copy, links and claims unchanged. *Why: owner said the old design felt generic, too busy/too plain, and not trustworthy enough for skeptical founders.*
- **Fixed response-time SLA claim site-wide:** "We respond within 4 hours" (contact page metadata/subheadline/panel, contact form trust signals x2, final CTA trust row, pricing tier's "Priority support" line, meta description table) was unrealistic for a 2-founder team — changed to "24 hours" everywhere. *Why: user corrected the claim directly ("we respond within 24 hours, not 4 hours, replace it everywhere").*
- **De-duplicated homepage content:** The same 4-stat block appeared 3 times back-to-back (Hero card grid → Ticker → Stats Strip) — removed Hero's stat-card grid, leaving Stats Strip as the sole formal stat block (Ticker kept, since it's a different visual treatment). The "five vendors blaming each other / one team / one system" pitch was restated near-verbatim in 4 sections (Hero, Problem Section, Multi-Channel, Services Grid) — kept it in Hero only and rewrote the other 3 to each make a distinct point (accountability/ownership for Problem Section, channel-coverage/competitive-positioning for Multi-Channel, operational-scope for Services Grid's End-to-End description). 5 separate full-button "book a free audit"-style CTAs appeared before the dedicated closing CTA — downgraded 4 of them (Problem Section, QC Spotlight, Marketplace Spotlight, Process Timeline) to inline text links, leaving Hero and Final CTA as the only full-button CTAs. *Why: user flagged that the homepage kept repeating the same stats and messaging throughout — each section should make one point once, not restate what an earlier section already said.*
- **Removed broken/unsubstantiated elements site-wide:** Hero's "Watch How It Works" button did nothing (no video existed) — removed. Contact page's Calendly iframe pointed at the generic calendly.com homepage, not a real booking link — removed along with the "book a 30-minute slot" copy; contact page now offers email + phone/hours only. Footer's Privacy Policy/Terms of Service/Sitemap links were all `href="#"` (dead) — built real `/privacy-policy` and `/terms-of-service` pages and pointed Sitemap at `/sitemap.xml`. Hero trust pills and ticker claimed "Shopify Partner," "Google Partner," "Meta Business Partner," "Amazon SPN Certified," and "Semrush Agency" — none of which are true for a 2-founder consultancy — reworded to specialization language ("Shopify & WooCommerce Specialists," etc.) that the team can actually defend. *Why: user flagged the non-functional video button and unsubstantiated partner/certification claims directly; auditing turned up the other dead links and the fake Calendly link in the same pass.*
- **Removed vanity scale stats, reframed around founder-led positioning:** Replaced "₹50Cr+ Revenue Generated" and "200+ Brands Launched" (hero, stats-strip, ticker, about, free-audit) with "2 Founders on Every Account" / "Founder-Led, Not Agency-Run" / "Direct Access to Your Strategists"; free-audit's "200+ Indian brands have gotten this audit" line reworded to "Every audit is done personally by one of our two founders." Updated `brand-context.md` stats table and added a "Team size / scale" note documenting the real state (2 founders, ~10–12 brands worked with) so future copy doesn't drift back to inflated numbers. *Why: the agency is a 2-person consultancy with 10–12 real clients — ₹50Cr+/200+ read as big-agency numbers that don't hold up and weren't true.*

- **brand-context.md:** Added "Live Status & Contact Facts" section (live domain, contact email, contact-form mechanism, WhatsApp/case-studies disabled status, D2C-framing and exclusivity-claim rules); fixed a stray "Indian D2C stores" stat wording to match the live "Indian ecommerce stores" copy. *Why: these facts existed only in code, not in any doc — no single source of truth for them.*
